Preview the full page without losing your focus. TabShow helps you find the right Chrome tab: hover to see it live, move away to return, or click to stay.

Get TabShow for Chrome: https://tab.show/
Chrome Web Store: https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/tabshow-live-tab-preview/njdjagodlomkhingeecipnlnnhnipkho

Search by title, URL or website. Use the arrow keys to select a result and Enter to open it. All windows searches windows in the same Chrome profile; separate Work and Personal profiles stay separate. Hover previews stay in the current window. Tabs in another window open when you choose them.

Free to use. No account. No tracking in the extension.
