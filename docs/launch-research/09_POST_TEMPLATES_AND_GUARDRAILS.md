# TabShow Post Templates and Guardrails

## Core Message

TabShow helps you find the right Chrome tab without switching away.

## Universal One-Liner

Hover any tab in a side panel to preview the live page, then click to switch or move away to snap back.

## Reddit Templates

### Builder Feedback Post

Title:

I built a tiny Chrome extension for people with too many tabs

Body:

I kept switching Chrome tabs just to check if I had the right page, then losing my place.

So I built TabShow. It opens a side panel, lets you hover any tab to preview the live page, then click to switch or move away to snap back.

I am deliberately trying not to turn it into a full workspace manager. No account, no backend, no browsing-data collection.

For people who keep 40+ tabs open: what would make this useful enough to keep installed?

### Competitor Discussion Comment

If you want to close tabs and save memory, OneTab is probably the right tool.

If you want project workspaces and sync, Workona is closer.

I built TabShow for a narrower problem: finding the right live tab without switching away from your current page.

### Privacy Reply

TabShow is local-first: no backend, no account, and no host permissions. The point is to preview/switch open tabs, not read page content or store browsing history.

## Guardrails

Do:

- Be honest that TabShow is narrow.
- Mention privacy clearly.
- Ask for feedback.
- Use real demos.
- Compare by job-to-be-done.
- Credit user suggestions in changelogs.
- Use Featurebase as the public feedback hub.

Do not:

- Claim it replaces every tab manager.
- Spam links.
- Overuse ADHD claims.
- Pretend to be a neutral user.
- Attack competitors.
- Promise AI, sync, or workspace features unless shipped.
