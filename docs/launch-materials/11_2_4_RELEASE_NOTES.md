# TabShow 2.4 release notes

Status: release candidate. Publish these notes with the verified release; they do not establish that 2.4 is already available in the Chrome Web Store.

## Keep the panel open

Open Settings and turn on **Keep panel open after choosing a tab**. Click a tab or press Enter, and TabShow stays open beside your page. The chosen tab becomes the starting point for your next preview.

This option is off by default. Chrome's pin icon only puts TabShow in the toolbar; the new setting controls whether it stays open after a selection.

## Keep your place in the list

Opening TabShow brings your current tab into view. Choosing a tab keeps your place in the list, and your sort order stays saved. Moving tabs between Chrome windows also updates the list correctly.

## Choose when to preview

**Preview tabs on hover** is on by default. Turn it off in Settings to browse the list without changing the visible page. Search, arrow-key selection, Enter, and clicking still work.

To keep previews but avoid accidental switches, increase **Hover preview delay** instead.

## Find version and help

Settings now shows your installed version and links to help and release notes. **Appearance → System** follows your light or dark preference. It does not copy a custom Chrome theme's colors.

Feature-release notes now open in a background tab. Patch updates do not open a release page automatically; the notes remain available in Settings.

Every feature remains free. The release is designed to keep the existing permissions and local-only tab-data handling; confirm these against the final package before publication.

## Short Store update

TabShow 2.4 adds an optional setting to keep the panel open after choosing a tab, an on/off switch for hover previews, saved sorting, and current-tab visibility when opening the panel. It also fixes list updates when tabs move between windows and makes version information and help easier to find. Existing panel and hover defaults stay the same.
