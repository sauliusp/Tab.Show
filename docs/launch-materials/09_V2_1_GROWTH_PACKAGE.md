I made a tiny Chrome extension for the “which tab was that?” problem

**Body**

When my tab strip gets crowded, I usually know the page when I see it — I just do not want to click through five wrong tabs to find it.

I built TabShow around that narrow workflow: open a searchable side panel, hover a tab in the current window to preview the live page, click to switch, or move away to snap back. It also searches across windows; tabs in another window switch only when clicked.

It is free, has no account or backend, and does not have host permissions. Here is a short real-product clip: `[attach pain cut]`.
