TabShow Version 2.4

What changed in TabShow 2.4

Switch tabs.
Keep the panel open.

You can now keep TabShow beside your page after choosing a tab. Your sort order stays saved, and opening the panel brings your current tab into view.

See how preview works ↗

Every feature is free. No new permissions.

01

Your choice

Leave the panel open.

Open Settings → Keep panel open after choosing a tab. Click a tab or press Enter, and the panel stays open. Choosing a tab in the current window makes it the starting point for your next preview.

This is off by default, so your existing workflow stays the same. Chrome’s pin icon only pins TabShow to the toolbar.

02

Less searching around

Your current tab in view.

When you open TabShow, the list starts at your current tab. Choosing a tab keeps your place in the list, and your preferred sort order is remembered.

Tabs also stay in the right window section when you move them between Chrome windows.

03

Preview when you want

Control hover previews.

Open Settings → Preview tabs on hover to turn live previews on or off. Previews remain on by default.

To keep previews but avoid accidental switches, increase Hover preview delay. Search and keyboard selection work either way.

04

Clearer settings

Help is easier to find.

Settings now shows your installed version and links to help and release notes.

Appearance → System follows your light or dark preference. Custom Chrome theme colors remain separate.

Still one shortcut away.

Suggested defaults: Command + Shift + X on Mac or Ctrl + Shift + X on Windows and Linux. If the shortcut is unassigned, paste this address into Chrome and set it beside TabShow.

↑↓ select · Enter choose · Esc return and close. With the panel kept open, a tab chosen in this window becomes your new starting point.
